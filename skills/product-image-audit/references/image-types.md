# Image types (the job an image does)

Pick ONE primary type per image, plus an optional secondary. A type is the buyer question the image answers.

| Type | Buyer question it answers | How to recognise it |
|---|---|---|
| **hero** | What is it? | Product alone, clean, usually first. Packshot or main colourway. |
| **variety** | What options are there? | Grid of flavours, shades, colours, sizes. Swatch cards. |
| **contents** | What do I get? | Everything in the box or bundle laid out. Pack sizes, servings, count. |
| **scale** | How big is it? | In a hand, next to a familiar object, dimensions drawn on, serving in a scoop. |
| **in_use** | How does it work in real life? | Product being used: applied, worn, poured, cooking, a dog eating it. |
| **texture** | What does it feel/look like up close? | Macro of the formula, fabric, finish, material. Swatches on skin. |
| **lifestyle** | Is it for someone like me? | Person or setting the buyer aspires to. Product present but not the focus. |
| **benefits** | What does it do for me? | Callouts or icons listing outcomes ("better sleep", "no bloating"). |
| **mechanism** | Why does it work? | How it works: diagram, layers, technology, "the science". |
| **ingredients** | What's in it? Is it safe? | Ingredient spotlight, supplement facts, materials, "free from" list, certifications. |
| **how_to** | How do I use it? | Steps, directions, routine, dosage by weight, sizing guide. |
| **results** | Will it work for me, and when? | Before/after, results timeline ("week 1, week 4"), clinical stats. |
| **social_proof** | Do other people like it? | Review cards, star ratings, customer quotes, UGC photos, "X sold". |
| **authority** | Who vouches for it? | Press logos, awards, expert/doctor/vet, certifications as a trust mark. |
| **comparison** | Why this over the alternative? | Us vs them table, vs the old way, vs competitors. |
| **founder** | Who's behind it? | Founder photo or story card. |
| **offer** | What's the deal and the risk? | Guarantee, free shipping, subscribe-and-save, bundle savings, trial. |
| **video** | (any of the above, in motion) | Video slot. Judge the job from the poster frame and say so. |

## Style tags (separate from the job)
studio · lifestyle · ugc (phone photo, real customer) · on_model · close_up · flat_lay · graphic (text-led infographic) · before_after · render (3D/illustration)

## Judgement notes
- An infographic that lists benefits over a lifestyle photo is **benefits**, style **graphic**. Job first, look second.
- A review card with a customer photo is **social_proof**, style **ugc**.
- "Clinically shown" stat cards are **results**, not benefits.
- A shade grid is **variety** even if it's beautiful.
- If an image tries to do 4 jobs at once (text everywhere), flag it as cluttered. It usually does none of them well on a phone.
