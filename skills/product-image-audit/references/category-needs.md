# What each category needs in its gallery

Must-have: missing it costs sales on almost every page in the category.
Should-have: most strong pages in the category have it.
Nice-to-have: helps, especially at higher prices or for cold traffic.

Every category: slide 1 carries the product AND one reason to buy. Social proof means real customers, not models. In crowded categories, comparison is at least a should-have.

Order guidance is a default. Change it when the product's biggest objection is different.

## Supplements (gummies, powders, capsules, greens, protein)
- **Must:** hero · benefits · ingredients (what's in it, supplement facts or ingredient spotlight) · results (what to expect over time, or study results) · social_proof
- **Should:** how_to (serving, when to take, taste/mixing) · comparison (vs pills / vs the leading brand / vs doing nothing) · authority (doctor, dietitian, third-party testing) · variety (flavours) · lifestyle
- **Nice:** contents (packs per box, servings) · offer (subscription savings, guarantee) · founder · scale
- **Order:** hero → benefits → results or proof → ingredients → how_to → comparison
- **Biggest objections:** does it work, how long until I feel it, is it safe, does it taste good.

## Skincare
- **Must:** hero · texture (formula close-up or swatch) · results (before/after or clinical %) · ingredients (hero ingredients and what they do) · how_to (where it fits in a routine)
- **Should:** in_use (on skin, ideally on several skin types/tones) · social_proof (UGC, review cards) · authority (dermatologist tested, awards) · benefits · comparison (crowded category)
- **Nice:** contents (size, how long a bottle lasts) · offer
- **Order:** hero → texture or in_use → results → ingredients → how_to → proof
- **Biggest objections:** will it work on my skin, will it break me out, what does it feel like.

## Makeup and lip
- **Must:** hero · variety (every shade, ideally on real lips/skin) · texture · in_use (on model, several skin tones)
- **Should:** results (wear time, before/after) · social_proof (real customers, not just models) · benefits (finish, ingredients as care) · how_to · comparison (crowded category)
- **Nice:** contents · authority · cross-sell (duo, routine)
- **Order:** hero → shade on skin → texture → variety grid → proof
- **Biggest objections:** will the shade suit me, what's the finish, does it last.

## Apparel
- **Must:** hero (flat or on model) · on_model front, back and side · close_up of fabric · scale/fit (model height and size worn, or a fit guide) · variety (colours, unless every colour is its own page)
- **Should:** lifestyle (worn in context) · benefits (feature callouts: pockets, stretch, fabric tech) · social_proof (real customers wearing it) · several body types and skin tones · comparison (vs other brands / vs regular fit, crowded category) · why the pack (multi-packs)
- **Nice:** offer (free returns) · how_to (care) · cross-sell
- **Order:** hero on model → back/side → detail/fabric → fit reference → lifestyle → proof
- **Biggest objections:** how does it fit, what's the fabric like, will it look good on me.

## Accessories and gadgets (wallets, bags, tech, tools)
- **Must:** hero · scale (in hand, in pocket, next to a phone) · in_use · benefits/features (what makes it different) · contents (what's in the box)
- **Should:** mechanism (how it works) · comparison (vs the old way) · close_up (materials, build quality) · social_proof · offer (warranty, guarantee)
- **Nice:** lifestyle · variety · authority (press)
- **Order:** hero → in_use or scale → key feature → comparison → contents → proof/warranty
- **Biggest objections:** is it really better than what I have, is it built to last, will it fit my stuff.

## Pet (supplements, food, treats, gear)
- **Must:** hero · in_use (a real dog/cat eating or using it) · benefits · ingredients (nutrition facts / guaranteed analysis, front and centre) · how_to (amount by pet weight)
- **Should:** results (timeline, mobility/coat changes) · authority (vet formulated, trainers) · social_proof (real owners with their pets, not staged) · scale (treat size, kibble size) · comparison (treats and supplements are crowded)
- **Nice:** offer · variety (flavours) · founder
- **Order:** hero → pet using it → benefits → ingredients → proof → how_to
- **Biggest objections:** will my pet eat it, is it safe, does it work.

## Home and kitchen (cookware, appliances, bedding, decor)
- **Must:** hero · in_use (cooking, on the bed, in the room) · scale/dimensions · variety (colours) · benefits/features
- **Should:** contents (what's included) · ingredients/materials (non-toxic, materials, thread count) · close_up · lifestyle · social_proof
- **Nice:** comparison · authority (press, awards) · how_to/care · offer (trial, warranty)
- **Order:** hero → in_use → features → size → colours → proof
- **Biggest objections:** will it fit my space, is it worth the price, what is it made of.

## Food and drink
- **Must:** hero · in_use (served, poured, in a glass/bowl) · variety (flavours) · ingredients/nutrition
- **Should:** benefits · how_to (recipes, how to make it) · social_proof (taste reviews) · contents (servings, pack sizes)
- **Nice:** comparison (vs soda, vs other brands) · lifestyle · offer
- **Order:** hero → served → flavours → benefits → nutrition → proof
- **Biggest objections:** does it taste good, is it healthy, how many servings.

## Anything else
Use: hero · in_use · scale · benefits · social_proof as must-haves. Pick the rest by asking "what is the biggest reason someone would hesitate to buy this?" and making sure one image answers it.
