# Credits

Product Image Audit was written from scratch. Before writing it we read and ran the closest public skills, to see what they cover and where they stop:

- `nexscope-ai/amazon-skills` (amazon-listing-images, amazon-product-photography): Amazon image-stack conventions.
- `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (page-cro): general page conversion audit.
- `autonnel/autonnel-skills` (landing-page-conversion-audit).
- `coleschaffer/copywritingskills-rmbc` (pdp-ecomm-template).
- `Liamrjohnston/landing-page-master-skill`: the blind-test method we adapted for our own testing.

None of them read a live product gallery or look at the actual images. That's the gap this skill fills. The category lists come from Ezra Rufino's 14 years building DTC product and landing pages.
